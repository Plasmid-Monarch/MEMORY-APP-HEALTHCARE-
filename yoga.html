🧘 Yoga Asanas

Gentle yoga for Mom - 10 to 15 mins each

Total Session: 75 mins - 6 Asanas

1. Tadasana - Mountain Pose

Stand straight, hands up, breathe deep for 10 mins.

10:00

2. Vrikshasana - Tree Pose

Stand near wall, one foot on other leg, hands together. 10 mins.

10:00

3. Sukhasana - Easy Sitting

Sit cross-legged, close eyes, slow breathing. 15 mins.

15:00

4. Bhujangasana - Cobra Pose

Lie on stomach, lift chest slowly, hold and breathe. 10 mins.

10:00

5. Pranayama - Breathing

Sit straight, breathe in left, out right - Anulom Vilom. 15 mins.

15:00

6. Shavasana - Relax Pose

Lie on back, hands open, full body relax. 15 mins.

15:00
